MR. BIG GUY — INFLUENCER, AFFILIATE, AND PODCAST PROMOTION LIST
Research prepared September 14, 2026

BOOK DETAILS TO USE IN OUTREACH
Title: MR. BIG GUY: The Rise and Fall of Curtis Slade
Author: Curtis Slade
Publisher: The Royal Book House
Format: Kindle Edition, 539 pages, English
Amazon listing: https://a.co/d/0eJcdXta

IMPORTANT CAMPAIGN NOTES
• Influencer promotion, review-copy distribution, affiliate marketing, and podcast distribution are different activities. The categories below are separated so money and effort are not spent on the wrong tool.
• Do not ask for paid or incentivized Amazon reviews. Give creators a complimentary reading copy only in exchange for an honest, voluntary review or disclosure-compliant content.
• Amazon affiliate links may only be used by creators who have their own approved Amazon Associates or Amazon Influencer account and follow Amazon’s current disclosure rules. Use the existing plain Amazon link unless a compliant affiliate link has been created.
• Podcast directories distribute a podcast RSS feed. They do not book Curtis as a guest. Use the guest-booking section for interviews and the directory section only if launching an MR. BIG GUY/Curtis Slade podcast.
• Confirm current pricing, genre rules, geographic availability, and each platform’s content policy before paying.

RECOMMENDED FIRST WAVE — BEST FIT FOR THIS BOOK

1. Bookinfluencers — Dedicated BookTok, Bookstagram, BookTube, and book-blogger campaigns
Website: https://bookinfluencers.com/for-brands
Best use: Hire or coordinate book-focused creators for a virtual book tour, review content, unboxings, excerpts, and short-form recommendation videos.
Why start here: It is specifically built for authors, publishers, and book campaigns, rather than general consumer brands.
Action: Create an author/brand campaign brief with the book cover, Amazon link, synopsis, audience, content requirements, compensation/copy terms, and disclosure expectations.

2. PodMatch — Podcast guest matching
Website: https://podmatch.com/
Best use: Build a Curtis Slade guest profile and request interviews with hosts whose listeners follow memoirs, personal transformation, hip-hop culture, Florida culture, crime/law, reentry, and street-lit discussions.
Action: Use a 100-word guest pitch, 3–5 discussion angles, book cover, Amazon link, and a short biography approved by Curtis.
Affiliate angle: PodMatch advertises corporate affiliate links; ask its team about the current program only if it fits the campaign.

3. Booksprout — Advance/review copy distribution
Website: https://booksprout.co/
Best use: Recruit readers for voluntary, honest reviews around launch or during a sustained review campaign.
Action: Confirm the title’s distribution and review-policy fit; never require a positive review.

4. BookSirens — Reader review-copy program
Website: https://booksirens.com/submit-book-for-review
Best use: Put the title in front of readers who opt into review copies by genre preference.
Action: Review eligibility and pricing, upload approved files/materials, and make all review requests voluntary and honest.

5. StoryOrigin — Author cross-promotion, review copies, and newsletter swaps
Website: https://storyoriginapp.com/
Best use: Collaborate with authors who serve adjacent audiences and use newsletter swaps, group promotions, and review-copy tools.
Action: Search for partners whose readers are interested in memoir, urban fiction, street literature, criminal-justice conversations, or Black cultural history.

6. TikTok One Creator Marketplace — Paid TikTok creator campaigns
Website: https://www.tiktok.com/business/en-US/tiktok-one
Best use: Source creators for disclosed short-form book content; prioritize BookTok, reading culture, memoir, and culture commentators over generic high-follower accounts.
Action: Build a campaign around an approved excerpt, the cover, strong hook lines, and the Amazon link. Request raw deliverables only if rights are explicitly included.

7. YouTube BrandConnect — Paid creator collaborations
Website: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813943
Best use: Longer interviews, book discussions, trailer/reaction content, and community-post promotion from channels with a relevant audience.
Action: Identify channels with consistent real engagement, then ask whether BrandConnect is available for the creator and campaign.

8. Bookshop.org Affiliate Program — Independent bookstore-aligned affiliate links
Website: https://bookshop.org/affiliates
Best use: Use only if MR. BIG GUY becomes available through Bookshop.org. Participating creators can share Bookshop affiliate links and support independent bookstores.
Action: Verify the title’s listing first. This is not useful for the current Amazon-only Kindle listing unless another edition is added.

9. Amazon Associates / Amazon Influencer Program — Affiliate links to the Amazon listing
Amazon Associates: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/
Amazon Influencer Program: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/influencers
Best use: Creators already approved by Amazon can generate their own disclosed affiliate link to the book listing.
Action: Do not distribute another person’s Associates link as if it belongs to each creator. Each participating creator should use their own approved tracking link and disclosure.

10. Reedsy Discovery — Editorial discovery/review opportunity
Website: https://reedsy.com/discovery
Best use: Submit for an editorial-style discovery/review listing if the current submission rules match the title.
Action: Treat this as reader discovery, not guaranteed sales or affiliate marketing.

OUTREACH ASSETS TO PREPARE BEFORE CONTACTING ANYONE

1. A short approved author biography (50 words) and a longer version (150 words).
2. The exact back-cover synopsis.
3. The official high-resolution cover file and approved image-use terms.
4. Three approved interview angles, for example: the book’s writing process; documenting a life story; power, betrayal, and survival as themes in the memoir.
5. A 30-second spoken introduction Curtis can use at the beginning of interviews.
6. A media kit containing title, author, publisher, publication date, format, ASIN, Amazon link, contact email, and cover.
7. A creator brief: required disclosure, approved talking points, prohibited claims, whether a free review copy is offered, compensation, content due date, and link destination.
8. A tracking plan: separate tagged landing pages or creator codes for any destination the publisher controls. Use each creator’s own approved Amazon affiliate link if using Amazon affiliate marketing.

SUGGESTED 30-DAY EXECUTION ORDER

Week 1
• Build the press/creator kit and an approved outreach spreadsheet.
• Open campaigns on Bookinfluencers, Booksprout, BookSirens, and StoryOrigin.
• Create profiles on PodMatch and MatchMaker.fm.
• Make a list of 50 relevant BookTok/Bookstagram/YouTube/podcast targets.

Week 2
• Send personalized pitches to the first 20 podcast hosts and 20 book creators.
• Offer a complimentary reading copy where appropriate and permitted.
• Secure disclosure-compliant creator deliverables; do not demand reviews.

Week 3
• Publish approved creator content, track public links, and collect organic comments/questions.
• Continue podcast outreach in a second batch based on responses and audience quality.

Week 4
• Review creator performance: views, saves, comments, link clicks, sales indicators, podcast bookings, and earned review coverage.
• Renew only the channels producing relevant reader engagement.
