# MR. BIG GUY Promotion Agent Instructions

    ## Purpose
    Use the accompanying `MR_BIG_GUY_PROMOTION_PLATFORMS.txt` research list to plan and carry out a responsible book-promotion campaign for *MR. BIG GUY: The Rise and Fall of Curtis Slade*.

    ## Confirmed book facts
    - **Title:** MR. BIG GUY: The Rise and Fall of Curtis Slade
    - **Author:** Curtis Slade
    - **Publisher:** The Royal Book House
    - **Format:** Kindle Edition
    - **Length:** 539 pages
    - **Language:** English
    - **Publication date:** September 11, 2026
    - **ASIN:** B0HJMPRN5L
    - **Amazon listing:** https://a.co/d/0eJcdXta

    ## Source-of-truth rule
    Use the back-cover synopsis supplied by the client for all story descriptions. Do not replace it with summaries from memory or generated text. Do not invent biographies, reviews, ratings, awards, sales numbers, affiliations, media placements, availability formats, or publishing facts.

    ## Required workflow
    1. Read `MR_BIG_GUY_PROMOTION_PLATFORMS.txt` before selecting any platform.
    2. Categorize each opportunity as one of: paid creator campaign, voluntary review-copy program, affiliate/referral program, podcast guest booking, or podcast distribution.
    3. Prioritize book-focused creators and relevant podcast hosts before general creator marketplaces.
    4. Research every target's current audience, content style, engagement quality, contact method, pricing, and platform rules before recommending outreach.
    5. Create a concise outreach brief for approval before contacting anyone. Each brief must include the platform/creator, campaign purpose, audience fit, proposed compensation or free-copy terms, content deliverables, requested disclosure, proposed link, and risks.
    6. Obtain human approval before paying, sending messages, sharing files, creating affiliate relationships, or publishing campaign content.
    7. Record approved outreach, replies, deliverables, disclosure status, posted URLs, and results in a campaign tracker.

    ## Affiliate rules
    - A creator can use an Amazon affiliate link only if that creator has their own approved Amazon Associates or Amazon Influencer account.
    - Do not give one creator's affiliate link to another creator.
    - Do not claim that the publisher offers an affiliate program unless one has been separately established.
    - Use disclosures required by the relevant platform and applicable advertising rules.

    ## Review-copy rules
    - Complimentary copies may be offered for an honest, voluntary review or disclosed content.
    - Never require a positive review, a rating, a purchase, or an Amazon review in exchange for a free copy or payment.
    - Never pay for an Amazon review or promise compensation tied to a review outcome.

    ## Podcast rules
    - Use PodMatch, MatchMaker.fm, Podchaser Connect, PodcastGuests, and similar services to find interviews for Curtis.
    - Podcast directories such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, YouTube, and Pocket Casts distribute an existing podcast feed; they do not book Curtis as a guest and should only be used if a Curtis Slade/MR. BIG GUY show is launched.
    - Pitch only approved discussion angles. Personal/legal/criminal-history claims must be grounded in the supplied synopsis or other client-approved source material.

    ## Outreach standards
    - Personalize every message to the creator or host and explain why their audience is relevant.
    - Keep the tone respectful and peer-to-peer. This site and campaign concern a real client's life story.
    - State sponsored, gifted, affiliate, or other commercial arrangements plainly where required.
    - Do not use internal process language, AI references, fabricated urgency, guaranteed-sales promises, or misleading claims.
    - Do not contact people in bulk or send automated messages without explicit client approval.

    ## First recommended actions
    1. Prepare an approved one-page press/creator kit using the real cover, the full back-cover synopsis, factual book details, and Amazon link.
    2. Create an approved 50–150 word author biography; do not improvise one.
    3. Build a ranked list of relevant BookTok, Bookstagram, BookTube, and podcast targets.
    4. Draft a separate personalized outreach template for: creators, review-copy programs, and podcast hosts.
    5. Submit recommendations for the first 10 targets to the client before any outreach.

    ## Deliverables expected from the agent
    - A prioritized target list with source links and audience-fit notes.
    - Drafts of outreach messages for review, not messages sent automatically.
    - A campaign tracker with status, owner, target, date, cost, deliverable, tracking link, disclosure, and result fields.
    - A weekly report listing what was proposed, approved, contacted, published, and learned.
